# Metrash

Metrash is a Ministry of Interior (MOI) service for the public. The name covers two things: free SMS alerts for residents of Qatar, and the Metrash2 mobile app, where many MOI applications are made.[^moi-metrash-individual][^moi-family-announcement]

## What the SMS alerts cover

- Traffic violations.[^moi-metrash-individual]
- Expiry of your residence permit, driving licence and car registration.[^moi-metrash-individual]
- The Arabic terms on the same page add passport and ID card expiry, sent 14 days before the date.[^moi-metrash-individual]
- They also add messages when someone you sponsor enters or leaves the country.[^moi-metrash-individual]
- The same alerts cover your children under 18 and people on your personal sponsorship.[^moi-metrash-individual]

## How to subscribe

1. Open Metrash for Individuals on the MOI site.[^moi-metrash-individual]
2. Enter your QID number, mobile number, date of birth and language, which is Arabic or English.[^moi-metrash-individual]
3. Enter the verification code sent to your phone by SMS.[^moi-metrash-individual]
4. Wait for activation, which comes 24 hours after you submit the request.[^moi-metrash-individual]

The same form lets you change your number or unsubscribe.[^moi-metrash-individual]

## Other Metrash options

- Companies can subscribe through Metrash for Companies.[^moi-metrash]
- An additional number can be registered to receive the same SMS messages.[^moi-metrash]

## What you can do on Metrash2

MOI says the General Directorate of Passports offers all its services electronically through Metrash2 and the MOI website.[^moi-family-announcement] These are examples MOI lists on its service pages.

### Residency

- Renew a residence permit.[^moi-rp-renewal]
- Cancel a residence permit.[^moi-rp-cancel]
- Update your record after you get a new passport.[^moi-rp-passport]

### Visas and family

- Apply for a family residence visa.[^moi-family-residence]
- Apply for a family visit visa.[^moi-family-visit]
- Apply for a return visa after more than 6 months outside Qatar.[^moi-return-visa]

### Travel

- Make a leave notification before you travel.[^moi-leave-notification]

### Traffic

- Renew a driving licence.[^moi-driving-licence]
- Report a minor accident between two vehicles, under Traffic Services and then Accident Report.[^moi-minor-accident]

## Languages

A 2017 government report said Metrash2 offered more than 120 e-services in six languages: Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Indian and Urdu. That figure is old, so the current list may differ.[^mot-metrash-languages]

## Things that trip people up

- The mobile number must be registered with your provider in your own name.[^moi-metrash-individual]
- MOI calls Metrash an informative service and takes no responsibility if a message is late or does not arrive, so keep track of your own expiry dates.[^moi-metrash-individual]
- You can check document expiry dates yourself on the Other Inquiries page.[^moi-other-inquiries]
