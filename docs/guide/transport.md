# Getting around: Metro, buses and taxis

Public transport in Qatar is built around the Doha Metro, with the Lusail Tram and feeder buses connecting to it. Mowasalat, the government-owned company behind the Karwa brand, runs the public buses and Karwa taxis.[^vq-getting-around][^mowasalat-taxi]

## Doha Metro

There are three lines, and all of them meet at Msheireb:[^vq-metro]

- **Red Line:** from Al Wakra in the south to Lusail in the north. It also serves Hamad International Airport at Terminal 1.[^vq-metro]
- **Green Line:** from Al Mansoura in the east to Al Riffa Mall of Qatar in the west.[^vq-metro]
- **Gold Line:** from Ras Bu Aboud to Al Aziziyah.[^vq-metro]

There are two classes of travel: standard, which includes family carriages, and goldclub.[^vq-metro]

The metro runs from early until late, with a closure on Friday mornings for prayers. Hours change, so check the Doha Metro website or app before you rely on a late or early train.[^vq-metro]

## Tickets and Travel Cards

Buy tickets from the vending machine at any station. A Travel Card is meant for frequent riders and is designed to be topped up.[^vq-metro]

The same travel card works on the Doha Metro, the Lusail Tram, Metrolink and metroexpress.[^mot-turquoise] On the tram, tap in and tap out at the validators.[^mot-turquoise]

This page does not quote fares. Use the ticket options link on the Visit Qatar metro guide for current prices.[^vq-metro]

## Lusail Tram

The tram connects to the metro at Lusail QNB and Legtaifiya stations.[^mot-turquoise] Visit Qatar lists four lines and 25 stations.[^vq-getting-around]

## Metrolink and metroexpress

Metrolink is the feeder bus network that carries people to and from metro stations.[^vq-getting-around] Visit Qatar describes the Metrolink bus as free.[^vq-metro] Routes change, and Mowasalat added M307 and M308 between Al Sudan and Sports City stations from 10 May 2026, so check the Karwa Journey Planner app or call 4458 8888.[^mowasalat-home]

In certain areas you can also request a free shared ride to the closest metro or tram station, booked on the Karwa app.[^vq-getting-around] The Visit Qatar metro guide also lists a metroexpress ride share.[^vq-metro]

## Karwa buses

Karwa Bus is the public bus network, operated by Mowasalat.[^vq-getting-around] The Karwa Bus app lets you plan a trip, see the network and manage your smart cards.[^mowasalat-home] Mowasalat also lists all public bus routes on its website.[^mowasalat-public-transport]

## Taxis

Karwa taxis are turquoise and easy to spot.[^vq-getting-around]

- **Booking:** use the Karwa Taxi app, the website, or the 24 hour customer service centre on 4458 8888.[^mowasalat-taxi]
- **Cheaper option:** the Economy Service, run with Fox Transport, is available only in the Karwa Taxi app.[^mowasalat-taxi]
- **Help:** customer support runs 24 hours by phone, with WhatsApp as well.[^mowasalat-taxi]
- **Larger or private cars:** Doha Limousine books through the Karwa app, online, or on the same number.[^mowasalat-taxi]

Uber is also widely used.[^vq-getting-around]

## Apps to install

- **Doha Metro app:** for hours and service information.[^vq-metro]
- **Karwa Taxi app:** for taxis and the Economy Service.[^mowasalat-taxi]
- **Karwa Bus app:** for bus trips and smart cards.[^mowasalat-home]
