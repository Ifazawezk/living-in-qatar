Driving licence and traffic rules ​

The General Directorate of Traffic handles driving licences, and what you need depends on whether you are visiting or living here.[1]

Non-Qataris and GCC citizens need a legal residence permit in Qatar before they can get a Qatari licence.[1]

Driving as a visitor ​

  • If you are a visitor or foreign tourist with a valid international driving licence, you can drive on it for no more than six months. After that, go to the licensing authority for approval.[1]
  • If you live in a GCC country and hold a valid licence from there, you can drive on it for no more than three months when visiting Qatar.[1]
  • Your passport is used to confirm your date of entry.[1]
  • MOI's list also shows a temporary driving licence for tourists, visitors and foreigners, valid for three months.[1]

Moving here with a foreign licence ​

MOI does not describe a simple swap. If you came to work, hold a residence permit and have a licence from your own country, you are sent to a driving school to take a test that shows you can drive. A committee under the Traffic Department runs the test.[1]

  • If you pass, you get the licence.[1]
  • If you fail, you get one more try. After that, you take a full learning course at the school.[1]

Getting a Qatari licence through a driving school ​

Conditions ​

  • You must be at least 18 for a light vehicle or motorcycle licence.[1]
  • You must be at least 21 for a heavy vehicle licence or for construction and agricultural equipment.[1]
  • You must meet the physical fitness requirement and pass the driving test.[1]

What non-Qataris need ​

  • A letter from your sponsor approving the application and naming the licence type.[1]
  • Your passport and a copy.[1]
  • A copy of the sponsor's ID card, if the sponsor is a person.[1]
  • 3 recent colour photos.[1]

You then choose one of the approved driving schools.[1]

Where to go ​

Licences are handled at the General Directorate of Traffic and MOI Services Centers. The fees are listed on the same MOI page.[1]

The Traffic department's contact number is 2344444.[2]

Renewing your licence ​

  • You can renew online on the Metrash2 app.[1]
  • A lost or damaged licence can be replaced, and MOI charges the same fee as issuing one.[1]
  • The Other Inquiries page lets you check when your driving licence expires.[3]

Checking and paying violations ​

  • MOI's Traffic Services page lets you look up violations recorded on a vehicle.[4]
  • The MOI traffic violations site lets you look up and pay violations using the vehicle number, personal number or establishment registration.[5]
  • If you subscribe to Metrash SMS, you get a traffic violation alert.[6]

Fees for traffic services such as vehicle registration are on the Traffic Fee page.[7]

Reporting a minor accident on Metrash2 ​

If two vehicles are in a minor accident, you can report it on Metrash2 and not go to traffic investigation in person.[8]

  1. Take up to 4 photos of each vehicle, with one showing the number plate clearly.[8]
  2. Move the vehicles away from the road.[8]
  3. Turn on location services on your phone.[8]
  4. Sign in to Metrash2, select Traffic Services and open Accident Report.[8]
  5. Enter the details of both vehicles: vehicle number, QID number and mobile number.[8]
  6. Attach the photos, confirm the data is correct and submit the report for traffic investigation.[8]
  7. Wait for the SMS asking you to expect a second message.[8]
  8. After investigators review the photos, both parties get a text telling them to go to the insurance company or giving approval for vehicle repair.[8]

The Traffic Reports page lets you look up a road accident report or a vehicle repair permit.[9]

Traffic rules ​

The General Directorate of Traffic says the current traffic law was issued in 2007, and its page has a Traffic Signs section.[2]
