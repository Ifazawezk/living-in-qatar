# Help wanted

**Unverified, please help.** Nothing on this page is advice. These are gaps: facts a reader would want, which we left out of the guide because we could not read them on an official page.

If you can open the official page, send a [correction](https://github.com/Ifazawezk/living-in-qatar/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) with the link. A photo of a counter notice or a screenshot of an app is a helpful lead, but the guide still needs a public official page to cite.

## Most needed

- **Labour law text.** The [work page](./guide/work-and-labour-rights) rests on a December 2021 booklet from the National Human Rights Committee, plus a January 2006 English copy of the law for the gratuity amount, because we could not open the current law text or a Ministry of Labour page. Please help us check both against Law No. 14 of 2004 and its later amendments.
- **End-of-service gratuity.** The guide now gives the amount from Article 54 of that 2006 copy. Has Article 54 changed since then? Please link the current text.
- **Current minimum wage.** Is the 2021 figure still correct in 2026?
- **Metrash2 basics.** Where is the official download link, and how do you register or activate the app? The Ministry of Interior's Metrash2 pages did not load for us.
- **Licensed exchange houses.** Where does Qatar Central Bank publish the current list, so readers can check a licence? QCB's older registration pages now lead to an error page.
- **Rental law.** Law No. 4 of 2008 and its amendments: who registers a new lease, by when, what it costs, and what the law says about deposits.
- **Health card and QID.** News reports say a valid QID replaced the printed health card for residents in April 2026. We could not find this on a Ministry of Public Health, HMC or PHCC page.
- **Health insurance for residents.** The official pages we read describe the visitor scheme only. What applies to residents, and who pays?

## Arriving and residence

- Lists of visa-exempt countries, and how long each nationality can stay.
- Hayya fees, stay limits and requirements for each visa type.
- Whether a visitor can extend an on-arrival or Hayya visa.
- What to expect at Hamad International Airport beyond passport, ticket and insurance, including e-gates and customs.
- Who applies for a work visa, and whether both medical tests listed by the Ministry of Interior apply.
- Steps and fee to replace a lost or damaged QID, and the rule on reporting a loss.
- Who still needs an exit permit, and how the exit rules work today.
- Where the residency medical must be done, and what it covers.

## Family

- Whether a medical test abroad is still required for family residence. Two Ministry of Interior pages differ.
- The housing salary figure for family residence. Older and newer Ministry of Interior pages differ, and the guide shows both.
- The deadline to apply for residence for a baby born in Qatar.
- Whether a working mother can sponsor her children, or a working wife her husband.
- Attestation rules for marriage and birth certificates issued abroad.

## Driving

- Main traffic rules: speed limits, seat belts, phone use, child seats and the points system.
- Fine amounts.
- Which nationalities can convert a foreign licence without a course.
- Whether a resident with a QID can keep driving on an international licence.
- How long a licence is valid for non-Qataris.
- What to do after a serious accident.

## Money and housing

- Whether you must complain to your bank before going to Qatar Central Bank, and how long QCB takes to reply.
- QCB's consumer protection phone numbers. Two QCB pages list different numbers.
- A current list of what banks need to open an account. The official text we read dates from 2013.
- How to take a case to the Rental Disputes Settlement Committee.
- Kahramaa's account deposit, and how to close an account when you move out.
- Kahramaa's tariff rates, in a form that can be quoted.
- Whether Qatar charges VAT.
- A newer consumer price index release than June 2026.

## Getting around, phone and emergencies

- Metro and tram fares, Travel Card prices, and where to buy or top up a card.
- Exact metro and tram hours.
- Karwa bus fares, smart cards and the route list.
- Ooredoo home fibre details.
- Whether the Communications Regulatory Authority's complaint timings from 2019 and 2020 still apply.
- Separate numbers for police, fire and the coast guard, if there are any.
- Emergency numbers for gas leaks, sewage and roads.

## Holidays and schools

- Official list of public holidays, and how each date is announced.
- The rule for the date of National Sport Day, and whether it is one of the employer-set days for private-sector workers.
- Ramadan working hours for 2026 in the private sector and in government.
- The private school complaints form and enrolment guide on the Ministry of Education's site, which did not load for us.
