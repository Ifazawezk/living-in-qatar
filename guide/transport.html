Getting around: Metro, buses and taxis ​

Public transport in Qatar is built around the Doha Metro, with the Lusail Tram and feeder buses connecting to it. Mowasalat, the government-owned company behind the Karwa brand, runs the public buses and Karwa taxis.[1][2]

Doha Metro ​

There are three lines, and all of them meet at Msheireb:[3]

  • Red Line: from Al Wakra in the south to Lusail in the north. It also serves Hamad International Airport at Terminal 1.[3]
  • Green Line: from Al Mansoura in the east to Al Riffa Mall of Qatar in the west.[3]
  • Gold Line: from Ras Bu Aboud to Al Aziziyah.[3]

There are two classes of travel: standard, which includes family carriages, and goldclub.[3]

The metro runs from early until late, with a closure on Friday mornings for prayers. Hours change, so check the Doha Metro website or app before you rely on a late or early train.[3]

Tickets and Travel Cards ​

Buy tickets from the vending machine at any station. A Travel Card is meant for frequent riders and is designed to be topped up.[3]

The same travel card works on the Doha Metro, the Lusail Tram, Metrolink and metroexpress.[4] On the tram, tap in and tap out at the validators.[4]

This page does not quote fares. Use the ticket options link on the Visit Qatar metro guide for current prices.[3]

Lusail Tram ​

The tram connects to the metro at Lusail QNB and Legtaifiya stations.[4] Visit Qatar lists four lines and 25 stations.[1]

Metrolink and metroexpress ​

Metrolink is the feeder bus network that carries people to and from metro stations.[1] Visit Qatar describes the Metrolink bus as free.[3] Routes change, and Mowasalat added M307 and M308 between Al Sudan and Sports City stations from 10 May 2026, so check the Karwa Journey Planner app or call 4458 8888.[5]

In certain areas you can also request a free shared ride to the closest metro or tram station, booked on the Karwa app.[1] The Visit Qatar metro guide also lists a metroexpress ride share.[3]

Karwa buses ​

Karwa Bus is the public bus network, operated by Mowasalat.[1] The Karwa Bus app lets you plan a trip, see the network and manage your smart cards.[5] Mowasalat also lists all public bus routes on its website.[6]

Taxis ​

Karwa taxis are turquoise and easy to spot.[1]

  • Booking: use the Karwa Taxi app, the website, or the 24 hour customer service centre on 4458 8888.[2]
  • Cheaper option: the Economy Service, run with Fox Transport, is available only in the Karwa Taxi app.[2]
  • Help: customer support runs 24 hours by phone, with WhatsApp as well.[2]
  • Larger or private cars: Doha Limousine books through the Karwa app, online, or on the same number.[2]

Uber is also widely used.[1]

Apps to install ​

  • Doha Metro app: for hours and service information.[3]
  • Karwa Taxi app: for taxis and the Economy Service.[2]
  • Karwa Bus app: for bus trips and smart cards.[5]
