# Family visas

If you hold a valid residence permit for work in Qatar, you can apply to bring your wife and children to live with you, or host relatives on a family visit. Both applications are made on the Metrash2 app.[^moi-family-residence][^moi-family-visit]

The Ministry of Interior (MOI) changed the rules in an announcement dated 05/12/2023. Salary and housing conditions now depend on what your electronic work contract says.[^moi-family-announcement]

## Family residence for your spouse and children

### Who can apply

- You need a valid residence permit for work.[^moi-family-residence]
- Government and semi-government employees need a senior profession, shown in the employment contract or an official letter from the employer. They also need family housing from the employer or a monthly salary of at least QAR 10,000 as stated in the contract.[^moi-family-residence]
- Private sector employees need a technical or specialized (non-labour) profession shown in the contract. They also need a salary of at least QAR 10,000, or QR 6,000 with family housing provided by the employer and shown in the contract.[^moi-family-residence]
- An older MOI conditions page still gives QR 7,000 for the housing option. The 2023 announcement and the service page both say 6,000.[^moi-family-conditions-old][^moi-family-announcement]

### Who you can bring

- MOI's service page covers wives and children coming for the first time on a residence visa.[^moi-family-residence]
- A son must be no older than 25 and a daughter must be unmarried.[^moi-family-announcement]

### What you need

- A valid passport copy of the expatriate host.[^moi-family-residence]
- A document that proves the relationship.[^moi-family-residence]
- A Good Conduct Certificate (Police Clearance Certificate) for family members aged 18 and above.[^moi-family-residence]
- Health insurance that covers the stay and starts from the date of entry.[^moi-family-announcement]

### School-age children

Children aged 6 to 18 must be registered at a licensed school in Qatar, or you must attach proof that they are enrolled in education abroad, through the education platform run by the education ministry. MOI says this is required both when the residence permit is granted and when it is renewed.[^moi-family-announcement]

### How to apply

1. Submit the application on Metrash2 and upload the documents.[^moi-family-residence]
2. After your family arrives on a residence visa, they complete the medical checkup within thirty days, then fingerprints and the residency procedures.[^moi-visa-tips]

The next steps are on the [Residence permit and QID](./residence-permit-and-qid.md) page.[^moi-visa-tips]

## Family visit visa

MOI grants this visa for one month, and it can be extended for another five months for first degree relatives.[^moi-family-visit]

### Who can host

- Your profession must not be in the labour category.[^moi-family-visit]
- You need a monthly salary of at least QAR 5,000 shown in an accredited employment contract.[^moi-family-visit][^moi-family-announcement]
- The visitor needs health insurance for the period of the visit.[^moi-family-visit]

### Who can visit

- First degree: father, mother, wife, children.[^moi-family-visit]
- Second degree: brother, sister, grandfather, grandmother, grandchildren.[^moi-family-visit]
- Third degree: children of a brother or sister, and uncles and aunts on both sides.[^moi-family-visit]

The 2023 announcement says the visitor's age is not limited.[^moi-family-announcement]

### What you need

- A copy of the visitor's passport, valid for at least six months.[^moi-family-visit]
- A document that proves the relationship.[^moi-family-visit]
- Proof of family accommodation in the host's name, accredited by the relevant authorities.[^moi-family-visit]

### How to apply

Submit the application on Metrash2 and upload the documents.[^moi-family-visit]

## Staying longer on a family visit

- To stay more than one month, finish the medical checkup before the allowed period ends and apply for the extension.[^moi-visa-tips]
- MOI's Visa Services page has a Visa Extension / Fine Payment option.[^moi-visa-inquiry]
- A visa that is not extended before it expires brings a fine for each day of delay. The amounts are on the MOI tips page.[^moi-visa-tips]

## Newborn babies

MOI has a free visa for babies arriving with their mother at the border, for use with their residence permit procedures. The baby must be not more than 6 months old.[^moi-newborn-visa]

- Both parents' residence permits must be valid, and the wife must be on her husband's sponsorship.[^moi-newborn-visa]
- Bring the original attested birth certificate and the baby's valid passport.[^moi-newborn-visa]
- This is handled at the port of entry by Border Passports.[^moi-newborn-visa]
