# Housing and renting

If you rent a home in Qatar, two things tie together: your lease, which is authenticated through the Ministry of Municipality, and your electricity and water account with Kahramaa. Kahramaa's move-in form asks for that authenticated lease.[^km-move-in]

## Your lease

- Lease services are on the Ministry of Municipality website. You log in through the National Authentication System, open the e-services and choose the property lease contract services.[^mm-lease-guide]
- The Ministry's guide says renewals can be done online, without visiting a municipality office or carrying paper documents.[^mm-lease-guide]
- A landlord can give someone electronic authorisation to handle requests to register, change, renew or end a lease.[^mm-lease-guide]
- After the municipality approves a renewal, you can print the new certificate from "My requests". A link to it is also sent by SMS and email.[^mm-lease-guide]

## Moving in: your Kahramaa account

Kahramaa handles this through its Move to New Premises service. Have these ready:[^km-move-in]

- Your electricity number, and your water number if you have it.[^km-move-in]
- Your RNR number, which is the authentication number for your rental agreement issued by the Ministry of Municipality.[^km-move-in]
- Your QID number and expiry date, or your establishment ID, plus a copy.[^km-move-in]
- A copy of your rental agreement authenticated by the municipality. Owners do not need this one.[^km-move-in]
- Your lease start and end dates, payment frequency and rent amount.[^km-move-in]
- Your move-in date, email, mobile number, P.O. box and address.[^km-move-in]

The form lets either you or Kahramaa take the meter reading.[^km-move-in]

## Paying your bills

- On Kahramaa's Bill Payment page, select your electricity number to see what is due. The page lists credit card, debit card and Apple Pay as payment methods.[^km-bill-payment]
- Prices are not quoted here. Use Kahramaa's tariff page and its calculator to see the current rates.[^km-tariff]

## Prepaid service

Some customers can pay in advance instead of receiving bills. It is only for properties with smart meters, within size limits for the electricity breaker and water pipe.[^km-prepaid]

- You apply through the Kahramaa website or app, and the tariff is the same as a normal account.[^km-prepaid]
- Kahramaa says no deposit is needed. There is a monthly meter rental of QAR 5.[^km-prepaid]
- You get a warning when your balance drops to QAR 30, and service is cut if it reaches zero.[^km-prepaid]
- Switching back to a normal account is allowed only in certain exceptional cases.[^km-prepaid]

## Reporting a fault

- Kahramaa's emergency line runs 24 hours a day: call 991 inside Qatar, or +974 4449 4000 from abroad.[^km-contact]
- You can also message Kahramaa on WhatsApp at +974 3030 3991, or use the support form on its website.[^km-contact]

## Rental disputes

Qatar has a Rental Disputes Settlement Committee. The government services catalogue lists a Ministry of Justice service for attesting a power of attorney to act before it.[^sharek-rental-poa]
