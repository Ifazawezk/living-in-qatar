# Arriving and visas

How you enter Qatar depends on your nationality and your reason for coming. Visitors may get a visa on arrival or apply online through Hayya, while people moving here come in on a residence visa and finish their residency steps after landing.[^moi-visit-visas][^moi-visa-tips]

The Ministry of Interior (MOI) says visitor visa rules can change without notice, so check the rules for your nationality close to your travel date.[^moi-visit-visas]

## Ways to enter

### Visa on arrival or no visa in advance

- MOI says citizens of more than 95 countries can enter with a visa on arrival at Qatar's entry points, and that citizens of several countries are exempt from getting a visa beforehand.[^moi-visit-visas]
- The length of stay varies by nationality.[^moi-visit-visas] MOI's on-arrival page lists tourist categories of 90 days and 30 days, along with other categories such as a visa for GCC residents and a business visa with its own terms.[^moi-on-arrival]
- GCC nationals do not need a visa. Hayya says your passport or GCC ID is enough.[^hayya-home]

### Hayya e-visas

- Hayya is the online platform for tourist visas. MOI lists four entry permits on it: the tourist visa, the entry visa for residents of GCC countries, the entry visa for people accompanying GCC citizens, and entry by Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA).[^moi-visit-visas]
- You apply at hayya.qa or through the Hayya mobile app.[^moi-visit-visas][^hayya-home]

### Visas issued in advance

- These are visas you receive before you travel and present at the arrival port. MOI's list includes business visas and family (personal visit) visas, which have their own page: [Family visas](./family-visas.md).[^moi-visas-in-advance]

### Coming to live or work

- MOI's Work Visa on Company Sponsorship is for workers being brought to ministries, government bodies, semi-government organizations and private companies.[^moi-work-visa]
- MOI lists these conditions: attested educational certificates, an employment contract between you and the employer, a good conduct certificate from your home country attested by the Qatar Embassy there, and passing the medical test outside Qatar.[^moi-work-visa]
- If you enter on a residence visa, you must do the medical checkup within thirty days of entering. Fingerprints come after the medical, and then the residency procedures are completed. The [Residence permit and QID](./residence-permit-and-qid.md) page has the details.[^moi-visa-tips]

## What to have ready at the border

- A valid passport. The two MOI pages differ on how long it must stay valid: the visit visas page says at least three months, and the visa conditions page says not less than 6 months.[^moi-visit-visas][^moi-visa-conditions] Having six months or more meets both.[^moi-visa-conditions]
- A confirmed return ticket or a confirmed ticket to continue your journey.[^moi-visit-visas]
- Valid health insurance from a company registered with the Ministry of Public Health.[^moi-visit-visas]
- For some nationalities, a hotel reservation, cash or a credit card.[^moi-visa-conditions]
- If you are driving in, vehicle insurance. Visitors using the Abu Samra border complete the required policies electronically and can pre-register through Hayya.[^moi-visit-visas]

## How to check a visa

- MOI's Visa Services page lets you follow the approval of a work or visit visa with the application number, or look it up with the visa number, and print the details of an issued visa.[^moi-visa-inquiry]
- The same page has a Track Application Status option for the Qatar Visa Center.[^moi-visa-inquiry]
- For a Hayya e-visa, use Track your E-Visa with your entry reference number, or your passport number and nationality.[^hayya-track]

## Staying longer and overstaying

- Confirm how long your visit visa allows when you enter. MOI says a family visit gives you one month.[^moi-visa-tips]
- To stay longer on a family visit, finish the medical checkup before your allowed period ends and apply for an extension.[^moi-visa-tips]
- If the visa runs out without an extension, MOI charges a fine for each day of delay, up to a maximum. The amounts are on the MOI tips page.[^moi-visa-tips]
- The Visa Services page also has a Visa Extension / Fine Payment option.[^moi-visa-inquiry]
- You can look up overstay violations through the Overstay Violations Inquiry service.[^moi-overstay]
