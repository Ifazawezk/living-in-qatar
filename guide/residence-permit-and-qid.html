Residence permit and QID ​

If you come to live in Qatar, you arrive on a residence visa and then apply for your first residence permit (RP). The Ministry of Interior (MOI) handles this as a First-Time Residence Permit service, and the application form is signed by your sponsor, either a company or an individual.[1]

The fee for the ID card, known as the QID, is charged together with the residence permit, for the validity of the permit.[1]

How to get your first permit ​

  1. Do the medical checkup within thirty days of entering Qatar.[2]
  2. Have your fingerprints taken after the medical is finished.[2]
  3. Prepare the documents for your sponsorship type, listed below.[1]
  4. Submit them at the MOI Services Centres or Expatriate Affairs.[1]

Documents for company sponsorship ​

  • Labour contract attested by the Labour Department.[1]
  • Residence permit application form, printed and signed by the sponsor.[1]
  • Your original passport and your photo.[1]
  • A copy of the company's establishment ID.[1]
  • A blood group certificate.[1]

Documents for personal sponsorship ​

  • Residence permit application form, printed and signed by the sponsor.[1]
  • The original passport and a photo of the person being sponsored.[1]
  • A blood group certificate.[1]

The current fees are listed on the same MOI page.[1]

How to renew ​

  • You need the renewal form with the sponsor's authorized signature and stamp, the original passport, and the establishment ID of the company or the personal ID of the individual sponsor. The work permit and residence permit fees are paid at this stage.[3]
  • You can renew at the Expatriates Affairs Department or MOI Services Centres, and the service is also available on the Metrash2 app and the MOI e-services portal.[3]
  • MOI says a renewal for 3 years gets a 20% discount on the fee.[3]
  • To follow a renewal, use RP Renewal Tracking with the personal ID number of the sponsor or the RP holder.[4]
  • To check when your documents expire, the Other Inquiries page covers the ID card, passport, RP and driving licence.[5]

If your permit lapses ​

MOI says an expired residence permit must be renewed within 90 days. After that a daily fine applies, up to a maximum, and the amounts are on the MOI tips page.[2]

Lost, damaged or changed documents ​

  • For a lost card, MOI lists a Lost Report service among its official documents services.[6]
  • The Other Inquiries page has a smart card application form for residents that you can fill out and print.[5]
  • If you get a new passport because the old one expired or was lost or damaged, there is a service to update your record. Bring the old and new passports and the application form, plus a Lost Report if the passport was lost. It is available on the Metrash2 app, at MOI Services Centres and at Passport Headquarters.[7]

Leaving and returning ​

  • MOI has a free Leave Notification service for expatriates leaving the country on leave. It is available on the e-services portal, the Metrash2 app and at MOI Services Centres.[8]
  • The Exit & Entry Permits page lets you check the expiry date of a leave notification.[9]
  • If you stay outside Qatar for more than 6 months, you need a return visa, and it depends on your residence permit still being valid. The sponsor applies through Metrash2, the e-services portal, Expatriate Affairs or MOI Services Centres.[10]

Cancelling a permit ​

To cancel an RP, you and your sponsor both sign the application form, and the sponsor's personal ID or establishment ID is needed to verify the signature. It can also be done on Metrash2 and the e-services portal.[11]

Tracking an application ​

Use the Residency Permits inquiry page to follow an RP application with the visa number, or with the personal ID number of the sponsor, the agent or the authorized person. The same page has a Change Employer Application Enquiry.[4]
