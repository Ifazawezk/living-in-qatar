# Health care and insurance

Two public bodies provide most government health care in Qatar. Hamad Medical Corporation (HMC) runs the hospitals and specialist care, and the Primary Health Care Corporation (PHCC) runs the local health centres, while the Ministry of Public Health (MOPH) oversees the system.[^moph-visitor-guide] Private hospitals and clinics also operate alongside them.[^moph-visitor-guide]

## Health card

PHCC says citizens and residents can apply for a health card to use its health centres and HMC hospitals.[^phcc-health-card] With a valid card, consultations, non-emergency treatment and prescriptions at government pharmacies are subsidised.[^phcc-health-card]

The steps below are what PHCC and HMC publish on their own pages. Call PHCC on 107 before you go, so you know the current steps and fees for your case.[^phcc-contact]

### How to apply

- **Nar'aakom app:** PHCC's app lets you request a card for yourself or your dependents. You need a Tawtheeq (National Authentication System) account and a QID valid for at least one year.[^phcc-new-card]
- **At a health centre:** PHCC says to go to the centre nearest your home and fill in the forms. You will get a PHCC health file number.[^phcc-health-card]
- **Through HMC:** HMC says single residents can apply through PHCC or HMC facilities, and married residents should apply through PHCC only.[^hmc-health-card]

### What to bring

- **Adult residents:** valid QID, a 4 x 3 cm photo and a bank card for the QR 100 fee.[^phcc-health-card]
- **Children of residents:** valid QID, a 4 x 3 cm photo, the inoculation card for a first issue if the child was vaccinated in Qatar, and the QR 100 fee.[^phcc-health-card]
- **Domestic staff:** valid QID, a 4 x 3 cm photo, the sponsor's QID and a QR 50 fee.[^phcc-health-card]
- **Everyone:** a copy of a phone, water or electricity bill as proof of where you live.[^phcc-health-card]

PHCC accepts card payment only, no cash, and issues health cards during morning working hours only.[^phcc-health-card]

### Renewing or replacing

You can renew at any PHCC health centre or HMC hospital, or online through Hukoomi.[^phcc-health-card] PHCC lists a replacement fee for non-Qataris of QR 100 if the lost card is still valid and QR 200 if it has expired.[^phcc-health-card]

## Your health centre and family doctor

PHCC assigns your health centre based on your residency address, under its patient registration policy.[^phcc-new-card] In the Nar'aakom app you can ask to change your health centre or family physician, and book, reschedule or cancel some appointments.[^phcc-new-card]

Your family physician is the first step for PHCC services, and that doctor can refer you to clinics you cannot book directly.[^phcc-new-card] Dependents under 18 with a valid QID can go on your app account, and at 18 they need their own.[^phcc-new-card]

## Phone lines

- **107:** PHCC's line for appointments, including urgent or same day requests.[^phcc-new-card]
- **16060:** HMC's Nesma'ak Patient Contact Center, open 24 hours in several languages. Call at least 24 hours ahead to change or cancel. Missing a specialist appointment can mean cancellation and a new referral.[^hmc-appointments]
- **16000:** the National Healthcare Helpline, which also connects you to urgent consultation services.[^hmc-emergency]

## Emergencies and urgent care

For a life-threatening emergency, call 999 or go to the nearest HMC emergency department. The emergencies page lists them.[^hmc-emergency]

For something urgent but not life-threatening, HMC points to PHCC's Urgent Care Units, which are open 24 hours and need no appointment.[^hmc-emergency] PHCC lists the centres that have one.[^phcc-urgent-care]

## Health insurance for visitors

MOPH's mandatory health insurance scheme is written for visitors. MOPH's visitor guide says it began in February 2023, and that a visitor without suitable insurance pays the full cost of treatment.[^moph-visitor-guide] The law MOPH links for the health insurance system is Law No. 22 of 2021, which regulates health care services in Qatar.[^moph-insurance-registration]

- **Visitors who need a visa before arrival:** you must buy a policy first, because the visa is not granted without one.[^moph-insurance]
- **Visitors with a visa on arrival:** you are exempt for the first 30 days from entry, and you must buy a policy to stay longer. MOPH advises buying one anyway to avoid the cost of care.[^moph-insurance]
- **Hayya Card holders:** you need insurance for the rest of your card's period.[^moph-insurance]
- **Not covered by the rule:** transit passengers and temporary entry travellers. Visitor insurance also does not apply to GCC citizens.[^moph-visitor-guide]

MOPH gives the premium as QAR 50 per month, and the shortest policy is 30 days.[^moph-insurance-faq] The basic policy covers emergencies and accidents up to QAR 150,000, inside Qatar only, and does not cover non-emergency treatment.[^moph-insurance][^moph-visitor-guide] Buy it from a company registered with MOPH or from an approved international insurer.[^moph-insurance] For questions, call 16000 and choose extension 1.[^moph-insurance]

## Health insurance for residents

The MOPH insurance page and FAQ describe the visitor policy only, so they do not tell you what applies to residents.[^moph-insurance-faq] Ask your employer what cover you have, and check with the MOPH helpline on 16000, extension 1.[^moph-insurance]

## Medical exam for residency

Expatriates applying to live and work in Qatar go through a medical fitness exam run by the MOPH Medical Commission.[^moph-mc-residency]

1. Bring your original passport and a copy of your residence visa showing a mobile number.[^moph-mc-residency]
2. If your company is arranging this, it registers you through the MOPH website.[^moph-mc-residency]
3. MOPH lists the fee as QAR 100.[^moph-mc-residency]
4. Results go electronically to the Ministry of Interior's residency system.[^moph-mc-residency]

You can check your result on the Medical Commission page, for exams done at the Medical Commission in Mesaimeer, the Red Crescent in Mesaimeer, Karwa on Industrial Road, Al Ahli Hospital, Al Emadi Hospital and Doha Clinic Hospital. Allow up to 72 hours.[^moph-medical-commission] The main building in Mesaimeer opens Sunday to Thursday, 7 am to 7 pm, and the phone number is 4467 9111.[^moph-medical-commission]
