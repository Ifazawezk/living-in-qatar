Housing and renting ​

If you rent a home in Qatar, two things tie together: your lease, which is authenticated through the Ministry of Municipality, and your electricity and water account with Kahramaa. Kahramaa's move-in form asks for that authenticated lease.[1]

Your lease ​

  • Lease services are on the Ministry of Municipality website. You log in through the National Authentication System, open the e-services and choose the property lease contract services.[2]
  • The Ministry's guide says renewals can be done online, without visiting a municipality office or carrying paper documents.[2]
  • A landlord can give someone electronic authorisation to handle requests to register, change, renew or end a lease.[2]
  • After the municipality approves a renewal, you can print the new certificate from "My requests". A link to it is also sent by SMS and email.[2]

Moving in: your Kahramaa account ​

Kahramaa handles this through its Move to New Premises service. Have these ready:[1]

  • Your electricity number, and your water number if you have it.[1]
  • Your RNR number, which is the authentication number for your rental agreement issued by the Ministry of Municipality.[1]
  • Your QID number and expiry date, or your establishment ID, plus a copy.[1]
  • A copy of your rental agreement authenticated by the municipality. Owners do not need this one.[1]
  • Your lease start and end dates, payment frequency and rent amount.[1]
  • Your move-in date, email, mobile number, P.O. box and address.[1]

The form lets either you or Kahramaa take the meter reading.[1]

Paying your bills ​

  • On Kahramaa's Bill Payment page, select your electricity number to see what is due. The page lists credit card, debit card and Apple Pay as payment methods.[3]
  • Prices are not quoted here. Use Kahramaa's tariff page and its calculator to see the current rates.[4]

Prepaid service ​

Some customers can pay in advance instead of receiving bills. It is only for properties with smart meters, within size limits for the electricity breaker and water pipe.[5]

  • You apply through the Kahramaa website or app, and the tariff is the same as a normal account.[5]
  • Kahramaa says no deposit is needed. There is a monthly meter rental of QAR 5.[5]
  • You get a warning when your balance drops to QAR 30, and service is cut if it reaches zero.[5]
  • Switching back to a normal account is allowed only in certain exceptional cases.[5]

Reporting a fault ​

  • Kahramaa's emergency line runs 24 hours a day: call 991 inside Qatar, or +974 4449 4000 from abroad.[6]
  • You can also message Kahramaa on WhatsApp at +974 3030 3991, or use the support form on its website.[6]

Rental disputes ​

Qatar has a Rental Disputes Settlement Committee. The government services catalogue lists a Ministry of Justice service for attesting a power of attorney to act before it.[7]
