# Cost of living basics

This page sticks to figures published by official bodies. It does not give typical rents or grocery bills, because the official releases used here report how prices change, not what a home or a basket of shopping costs.[^npc-cpi-june-2026]

## Tax

- There is no personal income tax. The General Tax Authority says individuals pay no tax on salaries, wages or personal income.[^gta-investors]
- Excise tax raises the price of some goods. Tobacco products and energy drinks are taxed at 100 percent of the higher of the standard price or the retail price before tax.[^gta-taxes]
- Sweetened drinks are taxed by sugar content: QAR 0.77 per litre at 5 to 7.99 g of sugar per 100 ml, and QAR 1.06 per litre at 8 g or more. Drinks under 5 g, and drinks with only artificial sweeteners and no added sugar, are exempt.[^gta-taxes]

## How prices are moving

The National Planning Council's Consumer Price Index tracks price changes against the same month a year earlier and the month before. The most recent release found when this page was checked covers June 2026.[^npc-cpi-june-2026]

- The index reached 110.12 points (base year 2018), up 0.06 percent on May 2026 and 2.21 percent on June 2025.[^npc-cpi-june-2026]
- Compared with June 2025, food and beverages rose 12.74 percent, and housing, water, electricity, gas and other fuels rose 2.67 percent.[^npc-cpi-june-2026]
- Transport fell 0.92 percent over the same year.[^npc-cpi-june-2026]

The Council links the increase to regional challenges that affected international supply chains.[^npc-cpi-june-2026]

## Electricity and water

Kahramaa's tariff page has a calculator for electricity and water, so you can work out a bill from current rates. Moving in and paying bills are covered in [Housing and renting](./housing-and-utilities.md).[^km-tariff]

## Getting around

- Visit Qatar says you can use the Doha Metro all day for a maximum of QAR 6.[^vq-metro]
- You buy a travel card at station vending machines, and some retailers sell them too.[^vq-metro]
- Mowasalat runs the Karwa buses. Use its Karwa Journey Planner app to plan a trip, and see Transport for more.[^mowasalat-bus]

## Health card

Hamad Medical Corporation lists a health card fee of QR 100 for residents, adults or children, and QR 50 for Qatari and GCC citizens and for domestic staff. Manual workers applying for a new card are listed at QR 50.[^hmc-health-card]

Replacing a lost card costs a non-Qatari QR 100 if the card is still valid, or QR 200 if it has expired.[^hmc-health-card]

## Wages

The National Human Rights Committee's 2021 booklet gives the minimum wage as QAR 1,000 a month, plus at least QAR 500 for housing and QAR 300 for food if your employer does not provide them. Check the current amounts with the Ministry of Labour.[^nhrc-booklet]
