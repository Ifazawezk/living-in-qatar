Metrash ​

Metrash is a Ministry of Interior (MOI) service for the public. The name covers two things: free SMS alerts for residents of Qatar, and the Metrash2 mobile app, where many MOI applications are made.[1][2]

What the SMS alerts cover ​

  • Traffic violations.[1]
  • Expiry of your residence permit, driving licence and car registration.[1]
  • The Arabic terms on the same page add passport and ID card expiry, sent 14 days before the date.[1]
  • They also add messages when someone you sponsor enters or leaves the country.[1]
  • The same alerts cover your children under 18 and people on your personal sponsorship.[1]

How to subscribe ​

  1. Open Metrash for Individuals on the MOI site.[1]
  2. Enter your QID number, mobile number, date of birth and language, which is Arabic or English.[1]
  3. Enter the verification code sent to your phone by SMS.[1]
  4. Wait for activation, which comes 24 hours after you submit the request.[1]

The same form lets you change your number or unsubscribe.[1]

Other Metrash options ​

  • Companies can subscribe through Metrash for Companies.[3]
  • An additional number can be registered to receive the same SMS messages.[3]

What you can do on Metrash2 ​

MOI says the General Directorate of Passports offers all its services electronically through Metrash2 and the MOI website.[2] These are examples MOI lists on its service pages.

Residency ​

  • Renew a residence permit.[4]
  • Cancel a residence permit.[5]
  • Update your record after you get a new passport.[6]

Visas and family ​

  • Apply for a family residence visa.[7]
  • Apply for a family visit visa.[8]
  • Apply for a return visa after more than 6 months outside Qatar.[9]

Travel ​

  • Make a leave notification before you travel.[10]

Traffic ​

  • Renew a driving licence.[11]
  • Report a minor accident between two vehicles, under Traffic Services and then Accident Report.[12]

Languages ​

A 2017 government report said Metrash2 offered more than 120 e-services in six languages: Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Indian and Urdu. That figure is old, so the current list may differ.[13]

Things that trip people up ​

  • The mobile number must be registered with your provider in your own name.[1]
  • MOI calls Metrash an informative service and takes no responsibility if a message is late or does not arrive, so keep track of your own expiry dates.[1]
  • You can check document expiry dates yourself on the Other Inquiries page.[14]
