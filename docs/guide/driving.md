# Driving licence and traffic rules

The General Directorate of Traffic handles driving licences, and what you need depends on whether you are visiting or living here.[^moi-driving-licence]

Non-Qataris and GCC citizens need a legal residence permit in Qatar before they can get a Qatari licence.[^moi-driving-licence]

## Driving as a visitor

- If you are a visitor or foreign tourist with a valid international driving licence, you can drive on it for no more than six months. After that, go to the licensing authority for approval.[^moi-driving-licence]
- If you live in a GCC country and hold a valid licence from there, you can drive on it for no more than three months when visiting Qatar.[^moi-driving-licence]
- Your passport is used to confirm your date of entry.[^moi-driving-licence]
- MOI's list also shows a temporary driving licence for tourists, visitors and foreigners, valid for three months.[^moi-driving-licence]

## Moving here with a foreign licence

MOI does not describe a simple swap. If you came to work, hold a residence permit and have a licence from your own country, you are sent to a driving school to take a test that shows you can drive. A committee under the Traffic Department runs the test.[^moi-driving-licence]

- If you pass, you get the licence.[^moi-driving-licence]
- If you fail, you get one more try. After that, you take a full learning course at the school.[^moi-driving-licence]

## Getting a Qatari licence through a driving school

### Conditions

- You must be at least 18 for a light vehicle or motorcycle licence.[^moi-driving-licence]
- You must be at least 21 for a heavy vehicle licence or for construction and agricultural equipment.[^moi-driving-licence]
- You must meet the physical fitness requirement and pass the driving test.[^moi-driving-licence]

### What non-Qataris need

- A letter from your sponsor approving the application and naming the licence type.[^moi-driving-licence]
- Your passport and a copy.[^moi-driving-licence]
- A copy of the sponsor's ID card, if the sponsor is a person.[^moi-driving-licence]
- 3 recent colour photos.[^moi-driving-licence]

You then choose one of the approved driving schools.[^moi-driving-licence]

### Where to go

Licences are handled at the General Directorate of Traffic and MOI Services Centers. The fees are listed on the same MOI page.[^moi-driving-licence]

The Traffic department's contact number is 2344444.[^moi-traffic-dept]

## Renewing your licence

- You can renew online on the Metrash2 app.[^moi-driving-licence]
- A lost or damaged licence can be replaced, and MOI charges the same fee as issuing one.[^moi-driving-licence]
- The Other Inquiries page lets you check when your driving licence expires.[^moi-other-inquiries]

## Checking and paying violations

- MOI's Traffic Services page lets you look up violations recorded on a vehicle.[^moi-traffic-services]
- The MOI traffic violations site lets you look up and pay violations using the vehicle number, personal number or establishment registration.[^moi-violations-pay]
- If you subscribe to Metrash SMS, you get a traffic violation alert.[^moi-metrash-individual]

Fees for traffic services such as vehicle registration are on the Traffic Fee page.[^moi-traffic-fee]

## Reporting a minor accident on Metrash2

If two vehicles are in a minor accident, you can report it on Metrash2 and not go to traffic investigation in person.[^moi-minor-accident]

1. Take up to 4 photos of each vehicle, with one showing the number plate clearly.[^moi-minor-accident]
2. Move the vehicles away from the road.[^moi-minor-accident]
3. Turn on location services on your phone.[^moi-minor-accident]
4. Sign in to Metrash2, select Traffic Services and open Accident Report.[^moi-minor-accident]
5. Enter the details of both vehicles: vehicle number, QID number and mobile number.[^moi-minor-accident]
6. Attach the photos, confirm the data is correct and submit the report for traffic investigation.[^moi-minor-accident]
7. Wait for the SMS asking you to expect a second message.[^moi-minor-accident]
8. After investigators review the photos, both parties get a text telling them to go to the insurance company or giving approval for vehicle repair.[^moi-minor-accident]

The Traffic Reports page lets you look up a road accident report or a vehicle repair permit.[^moi-traffic-reports]

## Traffic rules

The General Directorate of Traffic says the current traffic law was issued in 2007, and its page has a Traffic Signs section.[^moi-traffic-dept]
